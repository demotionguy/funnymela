# FunnyMela - features & controls

**Controls:** joystick (ya W A S D / arrow keys), Run button (7 sec daud, 5 sec refill; PC par Shift), E ya Space = dukaan/jhoola/pole, V = camera, M = music, drag = camera ghumao / upar-neeche dekho.

**Story:** ladka/ladki partner ke saath bike se mela aata hai, parking ₹20, phir Mummy-Papa se bachte hue mela enjoy karna hai. Pakde gaye = pitai + game over.

**Level:** Normal (aasaan), Medium (Mummy-Papa tez), Hard (aur tez, Run zaroori).

**Mela:** 10+ jhoole (Chakri, Ghoda, Jahaz, Maut Ka Kuan, Udan, Break Dance, Toy Train, Haath Jhoola, Helicopter, Roller Coaster, Drop Tower, Tora Tora, Dragon, Takkar Gaadi), 6 shows (Jadu Show hat game, Kushti tap battle, Nautanki, Bhoot Bangla, Bandar Tamasha, Sheesh Mahal game), 30+ dukaane, 15 khane ke thele, 7 zameen par baithe dukandaar, balloon/khilone wale, patake bazaar (gate ke bahar) jisme patake jalane par dukaan me blast, fire gadi, nayi dukaan.

**Extra:** Dost Chintu (sirf 2 baar aata hai aur Mummy-Papa ko bulata hai), Lucky Spin, Daily Reward, Missions + Level/XP, 12 mela music tracks (30-35 sec me badalte), funny hawker awaazein (Voice Off/Low/Normal/High), Map, Shop (kapde/accessories jo character pehenta hai), Character customize, 2 Donation Poles (top 5 + latest donors), Premium Store (Razorpay), 6 policy pages, Settings slide bar.
